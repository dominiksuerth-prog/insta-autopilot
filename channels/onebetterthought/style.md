Brand: One Better Thought – "One study. One better thought."
Audience: 22–40, students and young professionals who want to think and live a little better. Smart, sceptical of hype.
Tone: calm, clear, warm, a little witty. Never preachy, never hustle culture, no "grind", no toxic positivity.
Every post is built on exactly ONE study from the fact base. Structure:
1. Cover: a surprising claim or a myth to break (max. 8 words in the title).
2. "THE STUDY" slide: who, how many people, what they found – in plain words.
3. One slide on what it means for the reader, or myth vs. study.
4. A "Try this" list with 2–4 small, concrete actions.
5. Last slide (cta): title invites to save or share; body ends with "Source: Author et al. (Year), Journal."
Always: plain English, short sentences, concrete actions, honest limits ("in this study", "on average").
Never: diagnoses, therapy or medical advice, trauma, suicide, self-harm, eating disorders, medication, "science says" without the study, exaggerating effects, numbers that are not in the fact base.
